Support

Chat Mate 華語 helps you learn Taiwan Mandarin by chatting with AI friends from Taiwan, in Traditional characters with pinyin when you need it. Each day there is a short practice, then a task chat that uses today's pattern, with friendly feedback in English. No sign-up or login: your learning data stays on your device.

FAQ

The microphone or voice input doesn't work

Voice input needs two permissions: Microphone and Speech Recognition. Your iPhone asks the first time you tap the mic. If you declined, open the iPhone Settings app → Chat Mate 華語, turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition, then return to the app.

You can always type instead. Speech recognition uses Apple's service; see the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up and restore?

  1. Go to Settings → Back up all data. The share sheet opens.
  2. Choose Save to Files, then iCloud Drive or another place you like.
  3. On a new phone or after reinstalling, go to Settings → Restore from file and pick that file. Before restoring, the app shows how many items will be added, overwritten or removed.

Restoring replaces all data on this device. The backup file is not encrypted and includes your chats, so keep it safe. We never receive your backup, so if you delete the app without one, your data can't be recovered.

Why do I see "You've used today's AI chats"?

To keep the app running smoothly for everyone, each device can use the AI about 150 times a day (every chat reply, sentence check and chat summary counts once). The count resets at midnight Taiwan time (UTC+8). After that, practice that doesn't need the AI, like vocabulary, flashcards and quizzes, still works.

Are my chats saved?

Your chat history is stored only on your device. What you send passes through our server to the AI to generate a reply, and our server does not store it. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Clear all data, or delete the app. This can't be undone, so back up first if you need to.
