## 商店頁文字（en-US）

**Name**（30 字內）
Chat Mate 華語

**Subtitle**（30 字內）
Speak Taiwan Mandarin daily

**Promotional Text**（170 字內，隨時可改、不用送審）
Three minutes a day: fix a past mistake, recall a pattern, say one sentence out loud. Then chat with a friend from Taipei or Tainan and use it for real.

**Description**
Learn the Mandarin people actually speak in Taiwan — in Traditional characters, with pinyin whenever you need it.

A short daily route
• Today's practice (about 3 minutes): fix a sentence you got wrong before, recall a useful pattern, and say one sentence into the mic.
• A real task: order a bubble tea with less sugar and ice, ask the way to the MRT, or chat about your weekend — using today's patterns. Read a short summary afterwards.

Friends, not teachers
Chat with Mei-ling, an office worker in Taipei who loves night markets, or Chih-wei, who runs a small café in Tainan. They talk like friends, in short beginner-friendly sentences, using Taiwan words like 捷運, 便利商店 and 悠遊卡.

Pinyin when you want it
• Turn on Pinyin in any chat to see it under every reply.
• Every word, example and dialogue in the lessons comes with pinyin.
• Tap a word in a chat to save it to your word list.

Feedback that fits English speakers
Corrections focus on what English speakers find hard: measure words, 了 and 過, word order, and 的／得／地 — explained in English.

Lessons for real life in Taiwan
Start with tones, initials and finals, then eight beginner chapters: introducing yourself, ordering food and drinks, the MRT and buses, convenience stores, asking the way, renting an apartment, seeing a doctor, and small talk with coworkers.

Speak and listen
Practice with speech recognition and listen to every sentence read in Taiwan Mandarin.

Your data stays on your phone
No sign-up, no account. Your progress is stored on your device, and you can back it up to iCloud Drive.
